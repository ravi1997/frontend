# SRS Index & Manifest

**Application Name**: Form Management Platform (Enterprise)
**Version**: 1.0.0 (Reconstructed)
**Status**: DRAFT

## 1. Scope

A high-performance, enterprise-grade form builder and management platform featuring drag-and-drop construction, complex logic rules, multi-level approval workflows, and AI-assisted generation.

## 2. Core Modules

- **Form Builder**: The WYSIWYG editor for creating forms.
- **Logic Engine**: Conditional visibility and validation rules.
- **Workflow Engine**: Post-submission actions (Approvals, Notifications).
- **Respondent Interface**: The public or private facing form filler.
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Insights into form performance.
