# SRS: Context

## 1. Project Overview

The **Form Management System** is a modern, feature-rich platform designed to simplify the creation, distribution, and analysis of digital forms. It caters to organizations needing a robust way to collect structured data from employees or external respondents.

## 2. Business Goal

To provide a self-service form infrastructure that reduces development time for new data collection workflows and ensures data consistency across the enterprise.

## 3. Scope

- **Form Builder**: Drag-and-drop interface for composing complex forms.
- **Form Distribution**: Public and private links for form submission.
- **Analysis**: Real-time dashboards and response management.
- **Administration**: User management and role-based access control.

## 4. Target Users

- **Creators**: Staff members building and managing forms.
- **Administrators**: IT/OPS managing system settings and users.
- **Respondents**: Internal/External users filling out the forms.
